# 03 Clinic Cadence

**Intended audience:** Independent veterinary clinic owners and practice managers — practitioners who manage pricing themselves without a dedicated finance team and who need a practical, repeatable approach rather than an enterprise workflow platform.

**Design posture:** Warm, credible, and practitioner-facing. Cream background (`#F6F3EE`), forest green accent (`#2B6645`), subtle warm tones. The typography and layout feel closer to a professional services brand than a SaaS tool. No sharp tech edges, no dashboard metaphors, no enterprise jargon.

**Positioning emphasis:** The cadence concept is central — pricing review as a professional habit, not a one-time project. FeeWatch is the primary product and the hero; the enterprise path is a quiet, secondary section near the footer. The copy is deliberately practical and avoids implying that FeeWatch changes fees, creates automatic recommendations, or causes outcomes.

**Key guardrails honoured:**
- FeeWatch is described as providing historical context, not as a price-setter.
- The causal caveat is present once, in plain language, embedded naturally in the FeeWatch section.
- Enterprise workflow is surfaced as a secondary option, clearly scoped, with planned writeback noted.
- No fabricated metrics, testimonials, or comparison claims.
