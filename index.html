FeeWatch by Vet INC

Keep your fees current. Build a pricing rhythm that fits your practice.

FeeWatch helps independent clinics stay on top of fee changes through a regular, manageable review routine—informed by the historical context in your own records.

Ask about FeeWatch

FeeWatch uses your clinic’s own data to support better-informed decisions. It does not set prices automatically or guarantee specific outcomes.

The practice

A regular pricing review is a professional habit, not a project

Most pricing problems in independent clinics come from the same place: fees go untouched for too long, then a one-time catch-up review is rushed. FeeWatch is built to support the opposite—a steady, manageable cadence.

1

Schedule a recurring review window

Set aside time on a consistent schedule—quarterly, semi-annual, or whatever interval fits your practice. The goal is regularity, not frequency.

2

Pull historical context from FeeWatch

FeeWatch analyses your clinic’s own sales, revenue, and visit records to show how prior fee changes relate to your historical patterns. Context, not conclusions.

3

Review with your team, using real information

Your pricing decisions involve clinical judgement, client relationships, and business priorities that no tool can weigh automatically. FeeWatch gives your team better information for that conversation.

4

Make the changes you decide to make

Your team updates fees in your PMS. FeeWatch is not a price-setter and does not write to your system. Your decisions, your timing, your records.

5

Repeat on schedule

The value of a cadence is consistency. The next review is already on the calendar before this one ends.

FeeWatch

Historical context for every review

FeeWatch studies the records your clinic already generates—sales, revenue, visit volume—to build a historical picture of how your fees have changed and what surrounded those changes. It does not draw causal conclusions; it surfaces the patterns in your own data so your review is grounded rather than guesswork.

FeeWatch provides historical context drawn from your clinic’s own records. It is not a causal proof of anything, does not make automatic pricing recommendations, and does not change any fees. Outcomes depend on many factors outside its scope.
  • 📈

    Your data, not benchmarks

    FeeWatch works with your clinic’s own records. There are no external comparisons, industry averages, or manufactured metrics.

  • 🕐

    Historical patterns in context

    See how fee changes have related to revenue and visit trends over time in your own practice, ahead of your review conversation.

  • ☐

    Supports the decision-maker

    FeeWatch gives your team a better starting point. The judgement and authority to change fees stays with you.

  • 📅

    Built for the review cadence

    Designed to sit comfortably inside a recurring review workflow, not as a one-time audit or an always-on alert system.

What this is, plainly: FeeWatch adds historical context to a process your team already owns. A regular review cadence with better information is more likely to produce sensible outcomes than an infrequent review without it—but the tool does not cause those outcomes, and no pricing tool should claim otherwise.
